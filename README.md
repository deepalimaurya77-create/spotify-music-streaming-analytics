# Spotify Music Artist Streaming Analytics

## Overview
Analyzed a dataset of 500 global music artists to uncover trends in top artists, genre performance, solo vs. collaborative streaming patterns, geographic distribution, and language reach. The project was built using two tools — Google Sheets and Tableau — to demonstrate both spreadsheet-based analysis and interactive dashboard visualization.

## Dataset
- **Source:** [Spotify Music Artist Streaming Analytics – Kaggle](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/srisyra02/spotify-music-artist-streaming-analytics)
- **Size:** 500 artists
- **Fields:** Artist Name, Sex, Country of Origin, Primary Language, Primary Genre, Artist Type, Debut Year, Total Streams, Lead Streams, Feature Streams, Solo Streams, % of Solo Streams, Collaborative Streams, % of Collaborative Streams

## Tools Used
- **Google Sheets** — data cleaning, pivot tables, and the QUERY formula for SQL-style aggregation
- **Tableau** — interactive dashboard with maps, trend lines, and comparative visuals

## Analysis Performed
1. **Top 10 Artists** — ranked by total streams
2. **Genre-wise Streams** — total streams aggregated by primary genre
3. **Solo vs. Collaborative Streaming** — average % split by genre, highlighting genres that rely heavily on collaborations (e.g., Filmi, EDM) vs. those that don't (e.g., Rock, Metal)
4. **Country-wise Artist Distribution** — world map showing artist count by country of origin
5. **Debut Year Trend** — total streams by artist debut year, showing a peak for artists who debuted between 2006–2015
6. **Geographic & Language Impact** — total streams broken down by primary language

## Key Insights
- Drake leads the dataset with 137,492.1M total streams, followed by Taylor Swift and Bad Bunny.
- Hip-Hop is the highest-streaming genre overall (2,126,055.1M), closely followed by Pop.
- Collaboration culture is genre-specific — Filmi (96% collaborative) and EDM (88% collaborative) rely heavily on features, while Rock (94% solo) and Metal (97% solo) are driven mostly by solo work.
- The United States contributes the most artists (265), followed by the UK (55) and Mexico (27).
- English-language streams dominate the dataset, followed by Spanish — reflecting the global reach of Latin and Reggaeton artists.
- Streaming activity peaks for artists who debuted between 2006–2015, aligning with the rise of streaming-first music consumption.
